King Kong vs. Godzilla 1962

Original title: キングコング対ゴジラ

“Will you win, Godzilla? Will you win, Kong? The battle of the century!”
1962-08-11Rated NR97 minTMDB 6.8 (363 votes)Language jaGross $12,550,000TMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
ActionFantasyScience Fiction

Directed by Ishirō Honda

Written by George Worthing Yates, Willis H. O'Brien, Shinichi Sekizawa

The advertising director of Pacific Pharmaceuticals, frustrated with the low ratings of their sponsored TV program, seeks a more sensationalist approach. He orders his staff to Faro Island to capture King Kong for exploitation. As Godzilla re-emerges, a media frenzy generates with Pacific looking to capitalize off of the ultimate battle.
