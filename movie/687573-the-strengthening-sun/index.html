The Strengthening Sun 1974

Original title: تيغ آفتاب

1974-02-19103 minLanguage faTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗
ActionCrimeDrama

Directed by Amir Shervan

Written by Ahmad Najibzadeh

Hojjat is a gendarme, and his brother Mansour is a smuggler. Mansour is sent to prison, and after his release he promises Hojjat that he will no longer engage in illegal activities. Mansour meets a prostitute named Mahboubeh and intends to marry her. A smuggler named Khalaf and his brothers plan to eliminate Hojjat from their path...
