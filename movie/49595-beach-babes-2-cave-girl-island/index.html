Beach Babes 2: Cave Girl Island 1995

“Primitive Paradise”
1995-12-02Rated R76 minTMDB 4.3 (13 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
ComedyScience Fiction

Directed by David DeCoteau

Written by Alexander Sachs, William Kelman, Mark Michelini

The Beach Babes are back... and better than ever! Attempting to return to their home planet, Xena, once again, crash lands the T-Bird ship - this time on a primitive island full of naughty nubile natives. Chased by rampaging dinosaurs and pursued by the evil megalomaniac, James T. Renford II, and his wise-cracking sidekick, Gund, the stranded space sirens have the island rockin'. In an effort to help out the hot heroines, one of the girl's pumped-up playmates rewires Renford's computers which accidentally sets off a chain reaction that will cause the island to explode in an ultimate cosmic climax!
