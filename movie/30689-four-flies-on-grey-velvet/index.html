Four Flies on Grey Velvet 1971

Original title: 4 mosche di velluto grigio

“When the flies start to crawl, so will your flesh...”
1971-12-17Rated PG104 minTMDB 6.5 (444 votes)Language itTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
HorrorMysteryThriller

Directed by Dario Argento

Written by Dario Argento, Luigi Cozzi, Mario Foglietti

Roberto, a drummer in a rock band, keeps receiving weird phone calls and being followed by a mysterious man. One night he manages to catch up with his persecutor and tries to get him to talk but in the ensuing struggle he accidentally stabs him. He runs away, but he understands his troubles have just begun when the following day he receives an envelope with photos of him killing the man. Someone is killing all his friends and trying to frame him for the murders.
