The Con Artists 1976

Original title: Bluff - Storia di truffe e di imbroglioni

“They'll do anything for a buck!”
1976-04-15105 minTMDB 7.1 (167 votes)Language itTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗
ComedyCrime

Directed by Sergio Corbucci

Written by Mickey Knox, Dino Maiuri, Massimo De Rita, Sergio Corbucci

Belle Duke, in order to get revenge on her former lover Philip Bang, organizes his jail break. But instead of Philip, it is the Italian Felice Brianza, AKAS Felix, who escapes. Now Felix is obliged to help Philip to escape. He succeeds and the two join to defraud Belle. The swindle plot become more complicated when Felix falls in love for Philip's daughter.
