Leeches! 2003

“They will bleed you dry!”
2003-08-19Rated R85 minTMDB 3.6 (27 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
HorrorThriller

Directed by David DeCoteau

Written by Michael Gingold, David DeCoteau, Gary Barkin

When a crop of doped-up athletes from the Lakecrest College swim team dive into the campus lake, the bloodsucking leeches below undergo a stunning transformation. Ingesting small amounts of the swimmers' steroid-laced blood, the slimy creatures grow to unusual size and set out to sink their teeth into an unsuspecting student population.
