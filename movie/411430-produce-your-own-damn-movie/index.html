Produce Your Own Damn Movie! 2011

“The Must-Have 3rd Installment In The "...Your Own Damn Movie" Series”
2011-09-13Rated NR300 minLanguage enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗
Documentary

Directed by Lloyd Kaufman

When it comes to producing, no one speaks with more authority than Lloyd Kaufman creator of The Toxic Avenger and founder of the longest-running independent film studio, Troma Entertainment. Over the years he has discovered talents such as Trey Parker and Matt Stone (South Park, The Book of Mormon) and Vincent D'Onofrio (Law &amp; Order: Criminal Intent) to name a few. Candid interviews, tips, tricks and tidbits scattered throughout the DVD give filmmakers practical tools for getting a movie shoot off the ground, keeping it afloat and seeing it through to the end - Lloyd Kaufman shows you how it's really done.
