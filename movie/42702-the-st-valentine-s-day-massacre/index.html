The St. Valentine's Day Massacre 1967

“The shocking truth about events leading up to one of the most violent days in American history!”
1967-06-30Rated NR100 minTMDB 6.3 (119 votes)Language enBudget $2,175,000TMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
CrimeHistory

Directed by Roger Corman

Written by Monte Hellman, Howard Browne

Chicago February 14th 1929. Al Capone finally establishes himself as the city's boss of organised crime. In a north-side garage his hoods, dressed as policemen, surprise and mow down with machine-guns the key members of Bugs Moran's rival gang. The film traces the history of the incident, and the lives affected and in some cases ended by it.
