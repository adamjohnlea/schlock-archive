The Frightening 2002

“If you don't fit in... you're dead!”
2002-04-01Rated R85 minTMDB 5.1 (20 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
Horror

Directed by David DeCoteau

Written by Matthew Jason Walsh

18 year-old Corey Peterson moves to a small town due to a trauma in their past. On his first day at Hallows End High School, he's approached by fellow newcomer Mason, who warns Corey about mysterious disappearances. Corey and Mason investigate the secret about the school and expose the dark truth about Hallows End and the missing students.
