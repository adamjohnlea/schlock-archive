Checkmate 1996

Original title: Prendilo...Senza Fiatare

“The next move is yours.”

Directed by Joe D'Amato, Salvo Di Liberto

Written by Robert Lyons

Mark Davis stars as a wealthy playboy who lives his life on his own terms. He's the kind of guy who always gets his way, in life and in love. All that changes when he runs into a gorgeous married woman (Alex Dane). Her rail-thin figure and invitingly wide mouth are more than he can resist and soon the two are engaged in a flirtation that threatens to blow up in both of their faces.
