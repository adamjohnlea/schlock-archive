Tarao Bannai 1978

Original title: 多羅尾伴内

“The Legendary Detective Returns”
1978-04-08104 minTMDB 9.5 (2 votes)Language jaTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗

Directed by Norifumi Suzuki

Written by Yoshitake Hisa, Kōji Takada

Baseball player Takatsuka is murdered mid-game in front of a large crowd. The autopsy reveals that he has been killed by a needle coated with a poison used by the Ainu tribe for hunting. The next morning, sports photographer Kawase is killed in the same manner. Kawase’s sister, Yuko, hires Taro Banai, the detective with seven faces, to uncover the truth.
