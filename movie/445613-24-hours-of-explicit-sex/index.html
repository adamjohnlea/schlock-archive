Directed by José Mojica Marins

Written by José Mojica Marins, Mario Lima

Director José Mojica Marins' biggest box-office film ever: Three young men make a wager on which one is able to have sex with more partners in a 24-hour period. The three men "hire" a gay "unsuspicious" judge and start a plain "rodox" porn movie.They take several women to a beach house where the contest takes place. The movie also claims to be the first to show bestiality, a female-dog (fake intercourse) and a male-horse (implicit intercourse).
