El tren expreso 1955

1955-05-2392 minTMDB 8.0 (1 votes)Language esTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗
Drama

Directed by León Klimovsky

Written by Eduardo Borrás, León Klimovsky, Luis Fernando de Igoa

Mario is a famous concert pianist affected by an obsessive neurosis. He leaves Paris, cancels his contracts and returns to Spain to devote himself only to music. On his way to Spain he meets a young widow, who Mario saves when she tries to commit suicide by throwing herself from a train.
