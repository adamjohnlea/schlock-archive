The Romance of the Vampires 1994

Original title: 吸我一個吻

1994-03-1793 minTMDB 3.8 (10 votes)Language cnTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗
ComedyDramaRomanceThriller

Directed by Ricky Lau Koon-wai

Written by Nam Yin, Joey Cheung Jo-ngai, Ricky Lau Koon-wai, Ricky Ng, Victor Yeung

Love & passion is the name of the game in this romatic vampire thriller. Instead of "I want your blood...", the heart-broken vampire in this story punishes himself by refusing to suck the blood out of his victims.
