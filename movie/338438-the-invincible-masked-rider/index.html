Written by Johnston McCulley, Gino De Santis, Guido Malatesta, Luciano Martino, Umberto Lenzi

In 17th century Spain, the evil, corrupt Don Luis intends to marry his ward, Carmencita, to his stepson, Don Diego. A peasant revolt, fuelled by a plague, complicates matters, as does the arrival of a Masked Avenger.
