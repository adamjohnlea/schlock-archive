Escape from El Diablo 1983

“She got him in... They better get him out!”
1983-01-01Rated PG90 minTMDB 5.3 (3 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
ActionAdventureDrama

Directed by Gordon Hessler

Written by Len Wechsler

Two young Americans, Pauli and Daniel, journey via motorcycle across the border into Mexico to visit a bar next to the infamous El Diablo prison. Pauli starts a fight with off-duty guards and is locked up. When Daniel realizes the corrupt guards are demanding a steep bribe, he rounds up their SoCal friends—including surfers and skateboarders—to stage a daring breakout.
