First Men in the Moon 1964

“H.G. Wells' Astounding Adventure in Dynamation!”

AdventureScience Fiction

Directed by Nathan Juran

Written by H. G. Wells, Jan Read, Nigel Kneale

The world is delighted when a spacecraft containing a crew comprised of the world's astronauts lands on the moon, but are shocked when the astronauts discover an old British flag and a document declaring that the moon is taken for Queen Victoria, proving that the astronauts were not the first men on the moon.
