Directed by Claudio Bernabei, Joe D'Amato

Written by Joe D'Amato

The film shows the love of two women, July and Helen, for the motocross racer Peter. The handsome July is a sponsor for the young Peter. An intimate relationship develops from this collaboration, although Peter's real love belongs to Helen.
