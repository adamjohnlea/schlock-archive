Animal Protector 1988

Action

Directed by Mats Helge

Written by Anders Nilsson, Mats Helge

Swedish animal rights action film. Extremely strange things are happening at a US military base under the command of Colonel Whitlock. Under the pretext of necessary animal testing, Whitlock is illegally developing chemicals for biological warfare. Top CIA agents Lomax and Santino are tasked with investigating the mysterious machinations Whitlock has planned to take over the world. At the same time, a group of fanatical animal rights activists decides to free the tortured animals from the laboratory. Thus, Whitlock's violence and the CIA agents' deviousness are countered by the hope and despair of the selfless animal rights activists.
