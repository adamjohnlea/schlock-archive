Gunsmoke 1953

“Hired Gunslinger In A Lawless Land!”
1953-05-04Rated NR79 minTMDB 6.6 (20 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗
Western

Directed by Nathan Juran

Written by Norman A. Fox, D.D. Beauchamp

Reb Kittridge is hired by the villans but turns to defend the rancher Saxon after learning the true situation. Kittrige wins Saxon's ranch with a cut of the cards but Saxon has other reasons for loosing the gamble. Telford and Lake try everything from bushwacking to wildfire starting to stop the Saxon/Kittridge herd of cattle from reaching the railhead.
