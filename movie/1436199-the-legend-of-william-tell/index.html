The Legend of William Tell 2006

2006-01-0186 minTMDB 3.0 (2 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗

Directed by Fred Olen Ray

Written by Eric Groff

The Grizzly Scouts are about to hold their William Tell badge trials when Eddie discovers a secret about the town's richest, most popular family, whose son also happens to be Eddie's bully and the favorite to win the contest. Eddie devises a plan with newcomers Deena and her two brothers in order to try and make things right.
