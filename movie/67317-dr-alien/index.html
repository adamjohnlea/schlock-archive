Dr. Alien 1989

“She has the cure for growing pains!”
1989-10-1290 minTMDB 5.1 (34 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
ComedyScience Fiction

Directed by David DeCoteau

Written by Kenneth J. Hall

When the mysterious Ms. Xenobia takes over the local college biology class, the dating life of supergeek Wesley Littlejohn takes a serious turn. Littlejohn agrees to be a lab rat for the professor's unusual vitamin research study. Now, thanks to some out-of-this-world supplements, he's been transformed into the campus stud. However, Xenobia has ulterior extraterrestrial motives.
