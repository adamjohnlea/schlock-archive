SILENCERS

103 minLanguage enBudget $8,000,000TMDB ↗ Where to watch ↗

Directed by Richard Pepin

No one seems to know the true identity of the legendary Men in Black (M.I.B's) cryptic characters with pale skin, black trenchcoats, fedoras, and hypnotic black eyes that threaten witnesses of UFO sightings. Special agent Rafferty will soon find out all about these mysterious Men in Black when he is thrown into a maelstrom of action and intrigue after the top secret cryogenic transport he is assigned to proctect is attacked. Eventually Rafferty learns that the M.I.B's are actually Macarbians from Orion Constellation, whose sole purpose is to use the portal they are building in collaboration with our government as a gateway mission to invade Earth. Can Rafferty and Comdor, a peace officer from the star system Pleiades save Earth?
