Horror

Directed by C. Courtney Joyner, David DeCoteau, Linda Hassani

Written by Matthew Bright

In another horror anthology edited from earlier Full Moon releases: in "Ascent from Hell" (Dark Angel: The Ascent), a young demon female escapes Hell and comes to Earth where she becomes the punisher of sinners; Infinite Evil (The Lurking Fear) follows a race of cannibalistic genetic creatures live below a cemetery and hunt humans for food; and in Evil Never Dies (Talisman), the Black Angel is summoned from his resting place to bring about the end of the world.
