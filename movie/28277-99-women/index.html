99 Women 1969

Original title: Der heiße Tod

“99 WOMEN... behind bars -- without men!”
1969-03-05Rated R99 minTMDB 5.4 (60 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗

Directed by Jesús Franco

Written by Anya Corvin, Manfred R. Köhler, Ornella Zanelli, Carlo Fadda, Harry Alan Towers, Javier Peres Grober

Female prisoners endure the horrors of drug abuse, prostitution and rampant sadism at an island prison. When an escape attempt goes awry, the fugitives discover that escaping can be as dangerous as remaining in the prison.
