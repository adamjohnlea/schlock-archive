A persistent nightmare about a 16th-century sorceress burned to death on a beach sends teenage Angela seeking enlightenment at a fortnight retreat on a beautiful Caribbean isle. All the inhabitants are part of Angela's inexplicable dream, and when professed witch Bethany begins luring others into her coven, Angela slowly uncovers the horrifying truth.
