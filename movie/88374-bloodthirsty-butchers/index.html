Directed by Andy Milligan

Written by Andy Milligan, John Borske

Barber Sweeney Todd and baker Maggie Lovett join forces to commit a series of brutal, gory murders in London with a little help from Tobias Ragg, an employee of the bake shop. They abduct customers from the barber shop and kill them, baking them into meat pies sold to the public.
