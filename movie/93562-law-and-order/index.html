Law and Order 1953

“From Dodge City to Tombstone...His Guns Were the Only Law!”
1953-05-13Rated NR80 minTMDB 5.7 (19 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
Western

Directed by Nathan Juran

Written by Inez Cocke, W.R. Burnett, D.D. Beauchamp, Gwen Bagni, John Bagni

Wild West lawman Frame Johnson arrests a notorious outlaw, but, after the locals threaten to break into the prison and hang the crook without a trial, Johnson gets fed up with injustice. He decides to put his days of gunslinging behind him in favor of a simple domestic life with his lover, Jeannie. The couple move to the supposedly idyllic Cottonwood — only to find that their new home has been overrun by a rogue rancher who has unfinished business with Frame.
