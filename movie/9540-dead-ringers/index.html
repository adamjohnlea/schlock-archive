Dead Ringers 1988

“Two bodies. Two minds. One soul.”
1988-09-23Rated R115 minTMDB 7.1 (1074 votes)Language enBudget $13,000,000Gross $8,038,508TMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
HorrorThriller

Directed by David Cronenberg

Written by Bari Wood, Jack Geasland, David Cronenberg, Norman Snider

Elliot, a successful gynecologist, works at the same practice as his identical twin, Beverly. Elliot is attracted to many of his patients and has affairs with them. When he inevitably loses interest, he will give the woman over to Beverly, the meeker of the two, without the woman knowing the difference. Beverly falls hard for one of the patients, Claire, but when she inadvertently deceives him, he slips into a state of madness.
