Possessed 2005

“Myth or reality?”
2005-02-08Rated NR85 minTMDB 3.3 (3 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗
Horror

Directed by David DeCoteau, David Schmoeller, Neal Marshall Stevens

Written by David Schmoeller, Matthew Jason Walsh, Neal Marshall Stevens, Charles Band

Three gothic stories regarding witchcraft and demonic possession: an evil witch returns to finish off the descendants of the witch hunters who burned her 300 years earlier; a demon disguised as a sweet old woman traps boarders' souls inside a book of paper dolls; and a man, haunted by his dead father's ghost, explores the dark and enticing world of black magic.
