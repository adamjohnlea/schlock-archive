Murders in the Rue Morgue 1971

“LOVE and MURDER are the two consuming passions of the Rue Morgue!”

CrimeHorrorMysteryRomanceThriller

Directed by Gordon Hessler

Written by Edgar Allan Poe, Christopher Wicking, Henry Slesar

In turn-of-the-century Paris, Cesar Charron owns a theater at the Rue Morgue where he performs the titular play with his wife, Madeleine Charron, who suffers dreadful nightmares. When several murders by acid connect to Cesar, the prime suspect of Inspector Vidocq would be Cesar's former partner, Rene Marot.
