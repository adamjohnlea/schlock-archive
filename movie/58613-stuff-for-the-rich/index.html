Stuff for the Rich 1987

Original title: Roba da ricchi

1987-11-13106 minTMDB 5.1 (100 votes)Language itTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗
Comedy

Directed by Sergio Corbucci

Written by Bernardino Zapponi, Bruno Corbucci, Gianni Romoli, Mario Amendola, Massimo Franciosa, Sergio Corbucci

3 episodes set in Monte Carlo. In the first one a priest is compelled to become the lover of a princess. In the second, a con woman tries to cheat an insurance man. In the third, a man has to deal with his wife's suicidal attempts.
