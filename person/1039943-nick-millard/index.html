Nick Millard was a director and writer, known for Criminally Insane (1975), Cemetery Sisters (1987) and Doctor Bloodbath (1987). He was married to actress/producer Irmgard Millard, and had two children. He died October 29, 2022 in Pacifica, California, at the age of 81.
