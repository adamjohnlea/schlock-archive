Fabrizio De Angelis (Rome, November 15, 1940) is an Italian film director, screenwriter and producer. In addition to being a director, he also worked as a screenwriter for almost all of his films. He became famous for the series of The Boy with the Golden Kimono films he directed. In some films he signed himself with the Anglophone pseudonym Larry Ludman.
