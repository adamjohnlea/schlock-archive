One Dollar Too Many 1968

Original title: I Tre Che Sconvolsero Il West

1968-08-1291 minTMDB 7.0 (4 votes)Language itTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗
ComedyWestern

Directed by Enzo G. Castellari

Written by Enrique Llovet, José María Rodríguez Méndez, Augusto Finocchi, Vittorio Metz

Suave gambler Clay Watson, cocky sharpshooter Moses Lang, and wily thespian Edwin Kean are a trio of criminals in the Old West. The motley threesome are forced to form an uneasy alliance in order to find $400,000 dollars in stolen money.
