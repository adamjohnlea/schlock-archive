Tumbleweed 1953

“The story of Jim Harvey...the savage terror he faced...the desert woman he loved!”
1953-12-01Rated NR79 minTMDB 6.2 (18 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗
Western

Directed by Nathan Juran

Written by Kenneth Perkins, John Meredyth Lucas

Jim Harvey is hired to guard a small wagon train as it Yaqui fighters surround a wagon train, so Jim Harvey, the man guiding the pioneers, steps out to negotiate. They ignore his pleas and overrun the wagons, leaving him for dead. He manages to escape to the nearest town, but there, the law accuses him of deserting the settlers. The townspeople try to lynch him, so he flees to a ranch owned by kindly Nick, who lends Jim a trusty horse named Tumbleweed that may just be fast enough to save the day.
