“Evil Beyond Evil...”
1999-06-22Rated R72 minTMDB 4.6 (27 votes)Language enBudget $250,000TMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
Horror

Directed by David DeCoteau

Written by Charles Band, David DeCoteau, Neal Marshall Stevens, Matthew Jason Walsh

On Mayday 1998 in the town of Dunwich, Massachusetts, Elizabeth gathers together a group of specially selected friends for a rather odd party. It turns out that she is the descendent of the malevolent Lilith, who was burned at the stake precisely three hundred years ago. Now Elizabeth hopes to resurrect her dreadful ancestor and has a specific — and murderous — need for the guests she's chosen.
