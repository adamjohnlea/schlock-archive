A Royal Christmas Tree

Romance

Directed by Fred Olen Ray

Written by Jeffrey Schenck, Peter Sullivan, Fred Olen Ray

Cassie has always enjoyed helping her grandfather Joe run the family Christmas tree farm, a staple of Harvest Falls. But unbeknownst to her, Joe has grown tired from working his whole life, and has quietly put the farm up for sale. When the handsome Prince James arrives in Harvest Falls, he quickly falls for Cassie - but his true reason for visiting could spell the end of their love before it has a chance to bloom.
