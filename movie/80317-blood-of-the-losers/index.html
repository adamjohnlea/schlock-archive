Blood of the Losers 2008

Original title: Il sangue dei vinti

2008-01-01108 minTMDB 6.2 (10 votes)Language itTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Where to watch ↗
DramaHistory

Directed by Michele Soavi

Written by Giampaolo Pansa, Dardano Sacchetti, Massimo Sebastiani, Michele Soavi

Inspired by Giampaolo Pansa's controversial book, the film traces the story of a family torn apart by political divisions at the end of the Second World War: a son joined the partisan brigades and a daughter, instead, joined the militia of the Republic of Salò.
