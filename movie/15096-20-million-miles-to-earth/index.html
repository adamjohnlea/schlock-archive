20 Million Miles to Earth 1957

“Space nightmares!”
1957-06-0182 minTMDB 6.2 (181 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
HorrorScience Fiction

Directed by Nathan Juran

Written by Christopher Knopf, Robert Creighton Williams, Charlotte Knight

When the first manned flight to Venus returns to Earth, the rocket crash-lands in the Mediterranean near a small Italian fishing village. The locals manage to save Colonel Robert Calder, the mission commander. A young boy also recovers what turns out to be a specimen of an alien creature. Growing at a fantastic rate, it manages to escape and eventually threatens the city of Rome.
