Nude on the Moon 1961

“A truly different adventure!”
1961-01-0170 minTMDB 4.2 (38 votes)Language enTMDB ↗ IMDb ↗ Trailer ↗ Where to watch ↗
ComedyFantasyRomanceScience Fiction

Directed by Doris Wishman, Raymond Phelan

Written by Jack Caplan, Judy J. Kushner, Doris Wishman, Raymond Phelan

Through an unexpected inheritance, a couple of male scientists travel to the moon via a rocket of their own design. There, they find a lush garden paradise rather than a barren wasteland. They're captured by (semi-nude) telepathic humanoids and taken before the Great Council, where the Moon Goddess decides they should be allowed to continue their experiments. One falls in love, the other seeks to return home.
